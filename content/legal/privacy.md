## Information We Do Not Collect

WorldElection does not require user accounts, login credentials, or personal profiles to browse election calendars, country hubs, or live results. We do not sell personal data or run third-party advertising networks.

## Local Browser Preferences

To preserve your display choice between **Dark (Indigo Default)** and **Light** mode without flashing between page loads, the site stores a single preference key (`we-theme`) in your browser's `localStorage`. This value stays strictly on your device and is never transmitted to third-party brokers.

## Essential Technical Logs

Standard web infrastructure may temporarily process aggregate request metadata (such as HTTP status codes, route path, and rate-limiting IP hashes) solely to defend live election-night endpoints against abuse and maintain uptime during high-traffic ballot counts.

## External Sources & Links

Election pages may reference official national electoral commissions, statistical agencies, or public legislative portals. Visiting those external websites is governed by their respective privacy policies.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data removal requests related to editorial correspondence, contact `editorial@worldelection.example`.
