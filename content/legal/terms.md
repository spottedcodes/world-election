## Informational & Civic Purpose

All schedules, electoral system summaries, projections, and results published on WorldElection are provided strictly for informational, journalistic, and educational purposes. Nothing on this site constitutes official legal notice, voter registration advice, or electoral commission certification.

## Fair Use & Attribution

Researchers, journalists, and educators are welcome to reference figures and visual summaries published on WorldElection with clear attribution to both WorldElection and the underlying primary electoral authority.

## Automated Access & Live Endpoints

During active election nights, live JSON telemetry endpoints are rate-limited to preserve reliability for human readers. Aggressive scraping or automated polling faster than published refresh intervals may be throttled without notice.

## No Warranty & Availability

While we work diligently to verify ballot dates and tallies against primary electoral bodies, schedules and preliminary vote counts remain subject to official revision, recounts, or constitutional rescheduling.

## Updates to These Terms

As WorldElection rolls out country hubs and historical archives in upcoming phases, we may update these terms and post the revised date above.
