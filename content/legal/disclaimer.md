## Primacy of Official Electoral Commissions

Only the relevant national electoral commission, constitutional council, or interior ministry certifies official election outcomes. Preliminary counts, media tallies, and exit estimates shown on election night remain provisional until formal certification is issued.

## Non-Partisan Presentation & Color Policy

WorldElection maintains strict editorial neutrality across all jurisdictions. Our interface brand color (violet) is intentionally decoupled from major political party identities, and default multi-series charts use an eight-color colorblind-accessible sequence (`--d1` through `--d8`). When covering specific political parties or coalitions, official or recognized party colors are applied strictly as data metadata.

## Timezone Clarity

Global election schedules involve overlapping national time zones. Unless labeled otherwise, WorldElection displays dates in the election jurisdiction's local polling calendar alongside Coordinated Universal Time (`UTC`) references for poll closings.

## Reporting Corrections

Notice an upcoming decree change, postponed polling date, or clerical discrepancy? Please notify our data desk at `editorial@worldelection.example` with a link to the primary government or commission notice.
